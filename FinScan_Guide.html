Running a Scan

Double-click run_finscan.bat (the only file you ever launch) and pick an option from the menu:

[1] Single stock Prompts you for one ticker (e.g. NVDA) and runs a full analysis on it.
[2] Group Choose [T]ickers (enter up to 20 symbols, comma-separated) or [C]ategory (pick one or more of the 18 categories by number, e.g. 3,12,17). For category scans you also choose [T]op Picks (faster, hand-picked leaders) or [F]ull Market (broader universe).
[3] Full market Scans the full investable universe across all 18 categories. Takes 25–35 min on first run, 2–3 min after that (cached).
[Q] Quit Exit without scanning.
📂 Results are saved automatically to the output/ folder inside your FinScan directory.

⚠️ Important — Buy or Strong Buy ≠ buy immediately

  1. A Strong Buy or Buy verdict means the stock scores well on fundamentals and technicals — it does not mean you should buy it right now at any price.
  2. Always check the chart first. If the price is sitting at or near 52-week highs, making higher highs on low volume, or extended far above key moving averages (50-day, 200-day) — don't chase it.
  3. Add it to your watchlist and wait for a 5–10% pullback, a consolidation, or a retest of support before entering a position.
  4. A great company at a bad entry price can still lose you money. Timing matters as much as picking.
  5. FinScan is a screening tool — it surfaces opportunities. Your job is to decide the entry. Always do your own due diligence.
