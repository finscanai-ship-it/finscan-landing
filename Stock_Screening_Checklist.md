# The 10-Point Stock Screening Checklist

**The framework FinScan uses to find undervalued stocks across 4,000+ tickers — every week.**

Use this when you're researching any stock manually. If a company fails 3+ of these, walk away.

---

## 1. Valuation — is it actually cheap?

- **P/E ratio** below 18 (or below the sector median).
- **EV/EBITDA** below 12 — better than P/E because it strips out debt and tax noise.
- **P/B (Price-to-Book)** below 3 — for capital-intensive industries, below 1.5.

> ⚠️ A low P/E alone is a trap. Always check it against the *sector*, not the whole market.

## 2. Profitability — does the business actually make money?

- **Net margin** above 10%.
- **ROE (Return on Equity)** above 15% — and stable for 3+ years (avoid one-off spikes).
- **ROIC (Return on Invested Capital)** above 10% — this is the real quality test.

## 3. Growth — is revenue moving up?

- **Revenue growth** ≥ 5% YoY (3-year average, not single-year).
- **EPS growth** ≥ 7% YoY.
- Revenue growing *faster* than expenses (operating leverage).

## 4. Debt — can it survive a downturn?

- **Debt-to-Equity** below 1.0 (below 0.5 for non-financials = safer).
- **Interest coverage ratio** above 5× — earnings comfortably cover interest payments.
- **Current ratio** above 1.5 — short-term liabilities are covered.

## 5. Cash flow — paper profits or real cash?

- **Free Cash Flow positive** for the last 3 years.
- **FCF margin** above 8%.
- Operating cash flow ≥ net income (if not, accounting may be flattering earnings).

## 6. Dividend (optional, but worth checking)

- **Dividend yield** between 2% and 6% — above 6% often signals a trap.
- **Payout ratio** below 60% — dividend is sustainable.
- **History** of 5+ years without cuts.

## 7. Insider activity — what does management know?

- Insiders **buying** in the last 6 months = good sign.
- Heavy insider **selling** with no buying = red flag.
- Look up on SEC EDGAR (US) or your local market regulator.

## 8. Analyst & momentum sanity check

- Average analyst target price **above current price** by 10%+.
- Stock **above its 200-day moving average** = trend confirmation.
- RSI between 30 and 70 — avoid extreme overbought or oversold.

## 9. Industry & moat

- Does it have a **durable competitive advantage**? (brand, network effect, switching costs, scale, regulatory)
- Industry **growing**, not in structural decline.
- Avoid commodity-only businesses unless deeply undervalued.

## 10. Red flags — automatic disqualifiers

- ❌ Auditor changed in last 2 years.
- ❌ CEO or CFO resigned suddenly.
- ❌ Three consecutive years of negative free cash flow.
- ❌ Goodwill > 50% of total assets (overpaid acquisitions).
- ❌ Frequent share dilution without matching revenue growth.

---

## How to use this checklist

1. **Score the stock** — count how many points it passes. 8+/10 = strong candidate.
2. **Re-check quarterly** — the metrics that mattered last quarter may have flipped.
3. **Diversify** — even a perfect score is one company; build a basket of 8-15.
